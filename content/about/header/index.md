Agricultural and environmental data are rarely simple. Field trials span sites and seasons, measurements repeat over time and space, disparate data sources must be collated and harmonized, and the data seldom meet the assumptions of textbook methods. I specialize in exactly these problems. With a PhD in agronomy, a co-authored textbook on applied statistics, and a decade of experience supporting research projects across the agricultural and environmental sciences, I offer statistical expertise grounded in real familiarity with how this research is done. I can help at every stage: designing efficient experiments, choosing and implementing the right methods (from generalized linear mixed models and nonlinear modeling to Bayesian approaches to multivariate methods), building new tools when existing ones fall short, and presenting your findings clearly and defensibly.
