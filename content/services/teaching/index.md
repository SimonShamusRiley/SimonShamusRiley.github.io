Statistical consulting solves the problem at hand. Teaching builds the skills to solve the next one yourself. I design and deliver training for research groups, departments, and graduate programs, from short focused sessions to multi-day courses.

Custom workshops and training seminars. These are built around what your group actually needs. That may be a hands-on coding workshop that takes participants from raw data to a finished analysis in R, SAS, or Stan. It may be a seminar on a specific method, such as power analysis, mixed models for repeated measures and unbalanced data, nonlinear models for dose-response experiments, or Bayesian methods. Or it may be a session on the principles of good statistical practice, including how to design experiments and how to report methods and results so they are clear, complete, and convincing to reviewers. I have delivered workshops and seminars on all of these topics for research staff and graduate students at the University of Florida, Aarhus University, and the International Institute of Tropical Agriculture. Sessions are practical and grounded in examples from agricultural and environmental research, so participants can apply what they learn directly to their own work. Workshops can be held remotely or in person.

Lecturing for established courses. I can also teach for existing university courses, either a single guest lecture or a larger share of the course. I have lectured in and helped develop the curriculum for a PhD course in biostatistical modeling for agricultural science at Aarhus University. I also served as a teaching assistant for a graduate course in experimental design and data analysis at the University of Florida, where I ran the weekly SAS training sessions. I am also a co-author of Applied Statistics in Biology: A Practical Guide Using SAS, R, and JMP (Wiley, 2025).

Fees

- Custom workshops and trainings: €300 per day, where one day is 8 billable hours. Partial days are prorated at €47.50 per hour. Both preparation and delivery time are billed. Trainings can range from 2 hours to 5 days. After an initial discussion of your group’s needs and the topics to be covered, I will provide a cost estimate. For in-person workshops, the client covers transportation and accommodation.

- Lecturing for established courses: €300 per teaching day, regardless of the length of the day. Preparation time is not billed. Transportation and accommodation are covered by the client.

- VAT: No Italian VAT is charged under Italy’s flat-rate tax scheme. For clients in other EU member states, invoices are issued under the reverse-charge mechanism, so any applicable VAT is accounted for by the client in their own country.

- Additional fees: €2 stand duty per invoice, where applicable.
