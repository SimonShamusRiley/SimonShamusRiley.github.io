Some projects need more than advice. If you lack the time, the specialized skills, or the in-house support to carry out an analysis yourself, I can take it on, working closely with you so the results reflect your scientific questions and hold up to peer review.

Data preparation. Real research data are rarely tidy. Multi-site, multi-season trials, long-term monitoring records, and data assembled from many sources often arrive with inconsistent formats, missing values, coding errors, and structural problems that can quietly undermine an analysis. I can clean, manage, and validate large, complex, and messy data sets, so the analysis rests on a sound foundation.

Analysis. I have performed analyses for more than 120 research projects across agronomy, entomology, plant pathology, forage science, forestry, ecology, and food safety. These analyses use methods including generalized linear and nonlinear mixed models, Bayesian and hierarchical models, multivariate methods, and ordinal regression. I work in R and RStudio, SAS, JMP, and Stan, and can work in whichever your group uses.
